# Project Brief

## Product

- One-line vision: Show companies what the NOOVOY AI Onboarding Agent does and get them into a pilot; later, give invited users access to their agents.
- Target users: HR leaders (phase 1); invited employees, HR specialists, managers (phase 2).
- Primary user outcome: An HR leader understands the agent in under two minutes and sends a pilot request.

## Scope

- Must ship (phase 1): TR/EN product page, pilot request mailto, invitation-only login notice, roadmap, partners, targets, mock pricing, team.
- Not in v1: login, accounts, forms, backend, live AI demo, CMS.

## Principles

- The page describes what exists (Onboarding Agent, TRL 4 in progress) and labels everything else as roadmap or target.
- Concrete copy about documents, sources, plans and HR review; no buzzwords.
- Ethics is a product feature here: source-grounded answers, "I could not find this", HR review, no surveillance.

## AI Position

The site itself uses no AI in phase 1. Copy describing the agent must match the Master PRD: AI answers only from approved company documents with citations, never makes employment decisions, never monitors or scores employees; HR reviews plans.
